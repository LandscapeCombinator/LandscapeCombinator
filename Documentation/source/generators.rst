Generators
==========

.. _generators:

A Generator is an interface for actors that create content in your level.
Examples of Generators include:

* :ref:`Landscape Spawner <landscape-spawner>` - Creates landscapes from heightmaps
* :ref:`Spline Importer <splines>` - Imports splines from OpenStreetMap
* :ref:`Buildings From Splines <buildings-from-splines>` - Creates buildings from splines
* :ref:`Roads From Splines <roads>` - Creates roads from splines
* :ref:`Landscape Texturer <landscape-texturer>` - Creates decals with satellite images
* :ref:`OGR Geometry <ogr-geometry>` - Imports vector geometry and visualizes areas
* ``LandscapeCombination`` - Groups multiple generators together
